Official Archive

“A permanent record of learning, innovation, and community.”

The ISEP Batch 1 Archive brings together all facets of the inaugural internship cohort into a single, permanent digital repository. Through real-world engineering sprints, collaborative problem solving, and intensive faculty mentorship, interns worked on cutting-edge projects and demonstrated remarkable dedication.

This platform serves both as an organized historical exhibition for visitors and a community space where interns, faculty, and well-wishers can leave their reflections. All media and certifications are stored with strict view-only protection to preserve their archival sanctity.
